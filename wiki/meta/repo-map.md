## Repository Navigation

The maintained folder map is [[docs/repository-layout|Repository layout]].

and [[examples/README|Examples]] for runnable programs and legal corpora.

## Wiki Support

Open the repository root as the vault. Notes remain under `wiki/`; their
supporting material is now grouped beside them:

- `wiki/.raw/`: immutable imported source material
- `wiki/_attachments/`: reusable note attachments
- `wiki/_templates/`: note templates
- `wiki/skills/`: repository copies of the wiki workflows
- [[wiki/workflows|Wiki workflows]]: the retained workflow reference

The September 2026 migration is recorded in `docs/repository-migrations.json`.
Research data, publication sources, and legal provenance retain their paths.
